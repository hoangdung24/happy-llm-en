Happy-LLM is a tutorial about large language models. [Datawhale](https://github.com/datawhalechina/happy-llm) wrote it in Chinese, and this site is the English translation of its chapters.

## Chapters

1. [NLP Fundamental Concepts](/chapter1-nlp-fundamental-concepts-en): what NLP is, how it developed, its core tasks, and how text representation evolved.
2. [Transformer Architecture](/chapter2-transformer-architecture-en): the architecture behind modern large language models.
3. [Pre-trained Language Models](/chapter3-pre-trained-language-models-en): BERT, GPT, and the pre-training approach.
4. [Large Language Models](/chapter4-large-language-models-en): scaling, emergent abilities, and representative models.
5. [Build Your Own Large Model](/chapter5-build-your-own-large-model-en): a hands-on guide to building and training a model from scratch.
6. [Training LLMs in Practice](/chapter6-training-llms-in-practice-en): the full training pipeline, plus a [preference alignment supplement](/chapter6-preference-alignment-wip-en) that the original marks as work in progress.
7. [LLM Applications](/chapter7-llm-applications-en): RAG, agents, and other applications.
8. [LLM Reinforcement Learning](/chapter8-llm-reinforcement-learning-en): GRPO, OPD, Search-R1, and ReTool.
