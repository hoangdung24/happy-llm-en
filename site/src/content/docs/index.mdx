Happy-LLM is a systematic tutorial on large language models, originally written in Chinese by [Datawhale](https://github.com/datawhalechina/happy-llm). This site is an English translation of its chapters.

## Chapters

1. [NLP Fundamental Concepts](/chapter1-nlp-fundamental-concepts-en) — what NLP is, its history, core tasks, and the evolution of text representation.
2. [Transformer Architecture](/chapter2-transformer-architecture-en) — the architecture behind modern LLMs.
3. [Pre-trained Language Models](/chapter3-pre-trained-language-models-en) — BERT, GPT, and the pre-training paradigm.
4. [Large Language Models](/chapter4-large-language-models-en) — scaling up, emergent abilities, and representative models.
5. [Build Your Own Large Model](/chapter5-build-your-own-large-model-en) — a hands-on walkthrough of building and training an LLM from scratch.
6. [Training LLMs in Practice](/chapter6-training-llms-in-practice-en) — the full training pipeline in practice, plus a [preference alignment supplement](/chapter6-preference-alignment-wip-en) (work in progress in the original).
7. [LLM Applications](/chapter7-llm-applications-en) — RAG, agents, and other applications.
8. [LLM Reinforcement Learning](/chapter8-llm-reinforcement-learning-en) — GRPO, OPD, Search-R1, and ReTool.

## Source

The original Chinese version lives at [datawhalechina/happy-llm](https://github.com/datawhalechina/happy-llm). Translation was produced with AI assistance — code blocks and reference citations are preserved verbatim from the original.
